Case study 1 · Creator and backend engineer, Oct 2023 to present

ShipMatrix

A B2B logistics automation platform I designed, built and run independently. It bridges inconsistent merchant exports and real warehouse work, then produces carrier-ready shipping data, so merchants never have to rebuild their ERP or OMS processes. In production at Align Technology.

Smart Adaptable Mapping (SAM)

Every business exports differently. A one-time, 18-question setup teaches the system a merchant’s data structure. Each later upload is reinterpreted against that saved model, including relationships like split addresses or missing quantities.

Warehouse intelligence

Picker-path optimization, configurable packing and item-handling rules, bulk fulfillment and order consolidation.

Carrier automation

Turns processed shipments into carrier-specific output for waybill workflows, including DHL and SMSA. Customers keep their usual exports.

Full ownership

Requirements, architecture, implementation and ongoing support, built from first-hand experience of the people doing the work.

Case study 2 · Configurable systems

One commerce backend, many businesses

A reusable backend built with Django, Django Oscar, and PostgreSQL for businesses with different products and fulfillment needs. Shared functionality is built once, while merchants configure their requirements through a custom-built management dashboard I designed and developed. It is being adopted by Yagarz and a second clothing startup.

Product customization

Option groups with bilingual labels, selection limits, per-choice price adjustments and 8 presentation styles. Jewelry, fashion or food, with no industry-specific rewrite.

Backend-owned pricing

Rules, market data and snapshots keep pricing and order-state decisions on the server, never on browser-supplied values.

Order workflows

Product-specific state machines, order snapshots, PDF and CSV invoices, email history and failed-payment auditing.

Arabic and English
