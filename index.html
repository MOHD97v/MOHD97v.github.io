ENGINEERING DEEP DIVE / SMART ADAPTABLE MAPPING

Smart Adaptable
Mapping (SAM)

Every business exports differently. SAM uses a one-time, 18-question setup to interpret data structures, values, and their relationships—then reinterprets each uploaded file against the saved model to produce accurate, carrier-ready data.
